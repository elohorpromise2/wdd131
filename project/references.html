Project Sources

References

The following sources were used for information and inspiration while creating the Abuja Street Food Explorer.

Information and Food Research

  • Encyclopaedia Britannica: General background information about Nigerian culture, food, and traditions.
  • Wikipedia: General reference information about Nigerian foods and their cultural background.
  • Nigerian Food and Culture Resources: General research about popular Nigerian foods such as suya, masa, kilishi, abacha, puff-puff, and grilled fish.
  • Personal Research: General research and knowledge about affordable street-food options commonly enjoyed in Abuja and Nigeria.

Project Inspiration

This project was inspired by the variety of affordable street foods available in Abuja. The goal is to provide students, visitors, and residents with a simple guide for discovering popular local foods.

Image Sources

The images used in this project were created or prepared for educational use in the Abuja Street Food Explorer project. Food images were selected to represent common Nigerian street foods, while the hero image was created specifically for this project.
